Android Guardian
USBLooking…for a phone on USB

Connect your Android phone with a USB cable

Android Guardian reads every installed app and its rights, compares them with public lists of known stalkerware, and looks for spy-like behaviour. Nothing is installed on the phone and nothing leaves this computer.

    It also checks by itself every few seconds.

    Ready to scan

    Takes one to three minutes. Keep the phone unlocked and plugged in. If the phone asks "Allow USB debugging?", tap Allow.

    One more switch, only needed to take rights away or protect the phone

    You can scan and remove apps without it.

        Switches the phone's Private DNS to a service that refuses to look up known stalkerware and malware servers, so even an app that was missed cannot report back. Android 9 or newer. Undo any time here or on the phone in Settings › Network › Private DNS.
