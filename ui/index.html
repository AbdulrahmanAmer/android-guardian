Connect your Android phone with a USB cable

Android Guardian reads every installed app and its rights, compares them with public lists of known stalkerware, and looks for spy-like behaviour. Nothing is installed on the phone and nothing leaves this computer.

    Protect this phone

    Switches the phone's Private DNS to a provider that refuses to resolve known stalkerware and malware servers, so even a missed app cannot phone home. Android 9 or newer. Undo any time here or in Settings › Network › Private DNS.

    When you are done, turn USB debugging off in Developer options on the phone.
    Advanced: VirusTotal check, threat-list update

    With a free key from virustotal.com, the scan also asks VirusTotal whether any sideloaded app file is known malware. Only the file's fingerprint is sent, never the file.

    Updates itself weekly.
