# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help people who may be frightened and in danger. Everyone taking part is expected to act accordingly.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are not technical or who are scared.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the people this tool serves.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Using this project, its issues or its discussions to help someone monitor another person without their knowledge or consent. Requests of that kind are closed and the account is blocked.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening an issue marked **conduct**, or privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile (Abdulraham Amer). All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed from the project.

Consequences range from a private written warning, to a temporary ban from interaction, to a permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition.
